Ek Android mobile game ko professional aur Google Play Store ready banane ke liye jo-jo screens aur features chahiye hote hain, unka complete blueprint:

---

### 📱 1. Core Screens ki Complete List (End-to-End User Flow)

#### 1. Splash Screen (Brand Identity)
* **Kya dikhega:**
  * Aapka Studio Logo / Name (e.g. *"Aapka Studio Presents"*).
  * Made with Phaser / Custom Engine logo.
  * Duration: 1.5 se 2 seconds with a smooth fade-in / fade-out.

#### 2. Loading / Preloader Screen (Android Booting)
* **Kya dikhega:**
  * Slime ka cute running/squishing loader animation.
  * Progress Bar (0% to 100%) jo game assets load karegi.
  * Niche fun gameplay tips/quotes (e.g. *"Tip: Jo darawna dikhe wo hamesha sach nahi hota!"*).

#### 3. Main Landing / Home Screen
* **Kya dikhega:**
  * Game Logo: **"SLIME FLIP"** (animated glow ke sath).
  * Center me Slime mascot (idle bouncing, blinking, tap karne par cute reaction).
  * **PLAY** button (bada aur inviting).
  * Quick access icons:
    * ⚙️ **Settings** (Sound, Haptics, Controls)
    * 🎁 **Daily Reward**
    * 👕 **Skins / Wardrobe** (Slime ke alag-alag roop)
    * 🏆 **Achievements / Leaderboard**
  * Top bar: Total Stars collected, Total Coins.

#### 4. World Map / Level Select Screen (Candy Crush / Mario Winding Path Style) 🗺️
* **Design & Flow:**
  * Ek vertical winding (curved) path jo bottom se top ki taraf jaata hai.
  * **5 Zones ka progression (Har Zone me 30 Levels = Total 150 Levels):**
    * **Zone 1: Crystal Cavern** (Levels 1–30) — Purple/Cyan cave theme.
    * **Zone 2: Ancient Machinery** (Levels 31–60) — Gears, moving saws, bounce pads.
    * **Zone 3: Celestial Sky Islands** (Levels 61–90) — Floating clouds, sky voids.
    * **Zone 4: Inversion Core** (Levels 91–120) — High gravity flipping gauntlets.
    * **Zone 5: The Neon Void / Final Gauntlet** (Levels 121–150) — Ultimate master trial.
  * **Level Nodes:**
    * Completed level: Level number + 1 to 3 stars earned.
    * Current level: Pulsing green ring (highlighted).
    * Locked level: Lock icon 🔒.
    * Boss / Landmark levels (har 15th aur 30th level par special big star/chest icon).
  * Map screen khulte hi camera automatically player ke **current unlocked level** par smooth scroll karke center ho jayega.

#### 5. Pre-Level Dialog / Level Card Popup
* Map par kisi level node par click karne par direct jump karne ke bajaye ek card pop-up:
  * Level Title (e.g. *"Level 4: Pahaad ki Chadhai"*).
  * Star Goals (e.g., ⭐ Complete Level | ⭐⭐ Beat in < 20s | ⭐⭐⭐ Zero Deaths).
  * Player ka Best Time & Fewest Deaths.
  * **"START"** button.

#### 6. In-Game Screen & HUD (Gameplay View)
* Game world (1280x720) with Mobile Safe-Areas (punch-hole camera aur notch se safe distance).
* Top HUD:
  * ⏸️ Pause button (Top-Right).
  * Level Title & Deaths Counter (Top-Left/Center).
* Ergonomic Mobile Touch Controls:
  * Left side: ◀ Left & Right ▶ buttons.
  * Right side: ⬆ Jump button + 🌀 Flip button (sirf Flip Zones me visible).

#### 7. Pause Menu (Overlay Modal)
* Gameplay pause hone par:
  * ▶ **Resume**
  * 🔄 **Restart Level**
  * 🗺️ **World Map**
  * 🔊 Quick Sound & Music Mute toggles.

#### 8. "Stuck / Need a Hint?" Popup (Rage-Quit Prevention)
* Level Devil type games me jab player ek hi level me 8–10 baar out ho jata hai:
  * Ek soft popup: *"Tough puzzle? Watch a quick video to reveal a Hint or Skip Level"*.
  * Isse player frustrate hokar game uninstall nahi karega aur monetization bhi badhegi.

#### 9. Level Complete / Victory Screen
* Level complete hone par:
  * **"LEVEL COMPLETED!"** celebration banner.
  * 3 Stars filling animation (sound effects ke sath).
  * Stats: Time Taken, Deaths Count.
  * Buttons:
    * 🔄 **Replay**
    * 🗺️ **Map**
    * ⏩ **Next Level**
    * 🎁 **Double Stars / Extra Coins** (Rewarded Ad button).

#### 10. Settings Screen / Popup
* **BGM Volume** (Slider / Toggle).
* **SFX Volume** (Slider / Toggle).
* **Vibration / Haptics On/Off** (Mobile me jump/death par vibration feel acchi deti hai).
* **Controls Mode** (Touch buttons size & position adjust karne ka option).
* **Privacy Policy & Terms** (Google Play Store compliance ke liye mandatory).
* **Restore Purchases / Credits**.

#### 11. Slime Wardrobe / Skins Shop
* Android retention boost karne ke liye:
  * Slime skins: Ninja Slime, Golden Slime, Crown/King Slime, Fire Slime, Ghost Slime.
  * Unlock via Stars collected ya in-game coins.

#### 12. Daily Login Reward Screen (7-Day Streak)
* Har 24 ghante me open karne par reward (Day 1: 50 coins ... Day 7: Exclusive Skin).
* Android retention (Day 1, Day 7, Day 30 retention) ke liye zaroori hota hai.

### ⚙️ 2. Android Ke Liye Technical Essentials (Jo Web Me Miss Hoti Hain)

1. **Android Hardware Back-Button Handling:**
   - Phone ka back button dabane par app achanak crash/close na ho.
   - Gameplay me ho toh Pause menu open ho; Menu me ho toh *"Are you sure you want to exit?"* popup aaye.
2. **Notch & Cutout Safe-Area:**
   - Aaj-kal ke Android phones 20:9 aspect ratio aur punch-hole cameras wale hote hain. HUD elements edges se thoda andar hone chahiye taaki camera hole ke niche na chupein.
3. **Offline Play (No Internet Required):**
   - Game 100% offline bhi chale.
4. **Haptic Feedback:**
   - Android vibration API integrate ho (Spike hit hone par sharp buzz, trampoline bounce par soft pop).
5. **Save State & Cloud Backup (Local Storage / Google Play Games):**
   - Player ka progress (unlocked levels, stars, deaths) safely save rahe.
6. **Monetization Hooks:**
   - AdMob Banner (menus me bottom me).
   - Interstitial Ads (har 3-4 level complete hone par ek ad).
   - Rewarded Ads (Level skip ya hint ke liye voluntary ad).
